## A note on regional terms

The same thing often has two names:

- **Pig** and **scraper** mean the same device. Saudi Aramco documents say scraper and scraper trap.
- **Block valve**, **mainline valve** and **sectionalizing valve** are used interchangeably.
- **Right of way (ROW)**, **wayleave** and **easement** all describe the strip of land for the pipeline, with slightly different legal meanings.
- **ERW** and **HFW** describe the same family of pipe. HFW is the modern term.
- **SSAW**, **HSAW** and **spiral** all mean helical seam pipe.
- **MAOP** is used for gas (ASME B31.8) and **MOP** for liquids (ASME B31.4).

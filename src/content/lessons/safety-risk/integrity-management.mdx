## The cycle

Integrity management is a loop, not a project:

1. **Gather and integrate data.** Design, materials, construction records, operating history, inspection results, surveys.
2. **Identify threats** to each segment.
3. **Assess risk** and rank segments (previous lesson).
4. **Plan and carry out integrity assessments**: inspection, testing or direct examination.
5. **Respond.** Repair, replace, reduce pressure or re-inspect sooner.
6. **Prevent and mitigate.** Improve coating, CP, inhibition, patrols, markers, valves.
7. **Measure performance** and feed the lessons back into step 1.

The interval between assessments is set by how fast the worst credible defect could grow to failure.

## Three ways to assess

| Method | What it does | Best for | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In-line inspection (ILI)** | An intelligent pig measures the whole pipe wall | Corrosion, dents, cracks, mapping | Line must be piggable |
| **Pressure test** | Proves the pipe holds a pressure above MAOP | Cracks and seam defects; unpiggable lines | Finds only defects large enough to fail; service interrupted; says nothing about smaller ones |
| **Direct assessment** | Surveys and models predict where damage is, then excavations confirm | Unpiggable lines | Samples the line rather than inspecting all of it |

ILI is the preferred method wherever the line can be pigged, which is why piggability is designed in from the start (module M11).

## What design must hand over

The integrity engineer of 2060 will rely on what the project hands over today:

- as-built alignment sheets and the pipeline GIS database,
- pipe mill certificates with heat numbers tied to location,
- weld maps, welding procedures and NDT records,
- coating, holiday test and CP commissioning records,
- hydrotest records and charts,
- a **baseline in-line inspection** soon after commissioning,
- the design basis: pressures, temperatures, corrosion allowance, design life.

<Callout type="field">
Missing records are a real integrity threat. Regulations in several countries now require operators who cannot produce traceable, verifiable and complete records for a pipeline to re-test it or reduce its pressure.
</Callout>
